# Reports

This folder will contain decision-ready outputs rather than raw exploratory work.

## Planned deliverables

- `executive_decision_memo.pdf` — two-page recommendation for Marketing and Finance
- `experiment_methodology.md` — design, assumptions, tests and limitations
- `interview_qa.md` — concise explanations of the analytical choices
- `tables/` — reproducible tables used in the dashboard and memo
- `figures/` — final charts with clear titles, units and uncertainty

## Reporting standard

Every recommendation must show:

1. the treatment and comparison group;
2. the outcome being evaluated;
3. the absolute effect and uncertainty;
4. the commercial assumptions applied;
5. the implied incremental profit or loss;
6. the sensitivity of the decision; and
7. any limitation that could change the conclusion.

No result will be labelled incremental unless it is based on the randomised treatment comparison.
